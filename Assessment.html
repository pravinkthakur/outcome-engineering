How scoring works

Each sub-item contributes with its own weight. Types: check = 0/1, scale (0–5) = value/5, percent (0–100%) = value/100.
Parameter compliance = Σ(weight × value) / Σ(weights of non-N/A items). We map the parameter index to scale 1–5 via a non-linear curve (0–25→1..2, 25–50→2..3, 50–80→3..4, 80–100→4..5).
