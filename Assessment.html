Scoring model
Parameter compliance = sum(weights of checked items). Index = compliance (0–100).
Pillar = average of parameter indices. Overall = weighted by pillar weights.
If any gate fails, overall ≤ 3. Caps: Lead time/Release <3 ⇒ ≤2.4; Proximity ≤2 ⇒ ≤2.9; Traceability ≤2 ⇒ ≤3.0.
